Modern Minimalist Penthouse

Manhattan, NYC Completed 2025 Residential 2,400 sq ft
Overview

A Sculptural Urban Sanctuary

Perched high above the Manhattan skyline, this 2,400-square-foot penthouse redefines urban luxury through a lens of minimalist sophistication. The design philosophy centered on creating a serene retreat that feels both expansive and intimate — a place where light, texture, and proportion come together in perfect harmony.

Every surface, material, and detail was meticulously curated to evoke a sense of calm and timeless elegance. From the floor-to-ceiling windows that frame panoramic city views to the hand-selected natural stone and warm oak flooring, the space breathes with an effortless, understated luxury.
